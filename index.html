A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

Build a 2D scan reading.

  1. Load a model. Select an OBJ, MTL and textures together, or choose their folder. GLB works alone; GLTF needs its .bin and image files.
  2. Shape the scan. Adjust density and edge definition on the left.
  3. Compose readings. Add up to eight high-definition layers on the right. Choose a treatment and direction for each, then adjust overlap, opacity and position. Link positions to move the whole arrangement together.
  4. Separate colors. Change the scan overlay palette without changing the page background. Custom two-color mode lets you choose both overlay endpoints.
  5. Finish vectors. Choose rectilinear planes, traced contours, or curved patches. Show fills, lines, or both; adjust structure reduction, region filtering, line weight, interior lines, and ink color. Turn the finish off for an exact-color sample-by-sample SVG.
  6. Set the page. Choose its ratio and pixel width, then scale and position the artwork on the page.
  7. Export. Save a PNG or SVG at the selected page dimensions. Open the vector editor to recolor or move traced regions and add graphic shapes before exporting an editable SVG.

These are CT-style graphics made from textured mesh surfaces. The mesh does not contain measured internal CT data. Imports stay in your browser.
